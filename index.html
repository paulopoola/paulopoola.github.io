Sheet Metal Bracket & Unfold Tooling Design

Objective: Develop a repeatable mathematical and CAD-integrated sheet metal unfold system for 2024-T3 aluminum structural brackets.

Execution: Built an algorithmic calculation engine factoring sheet thickness ($t$), inside bend radius ($R$), angle ($\theta$), and $K$-factor neutral axis placement ($K=0.33$).

Outcome: Eliminated shop-floor brake setup trial-and-error, guaranteeing precision sight line ($SL$) positioning and zero spring-back deviation.

Reverse-Engineered Structural Fitting (CATIA V5)

Objective: Reverse engineer a damaged primary structural airframe attachment bracket for legacy fleet support.

Execution: Performed precision caliper measurements, reconstructed 3D solids in CATIA V5 and 3DEXPERIENCE, and applied GD&T per ASME Y14.5.

Outcome: Delivered fully constrained parametric models with integrated internal fillets to alleviate fatigue stress concentrations.

Composite Layup Mold Design & FEA

Objective: Design high-temperature additive tooling for carbon fiber vacuum bagging and autoclave curing.

Execution: Modeled symmetric $[0/\pm45/90]_s$ quasi-isotropic ply schedules in SolidWorks surfacing and simulated thermal expansion differentials in ANSYS Mechanical.

Outcome: Prevented cure warping and achieved dimensional stability within $\pm0.005$ inches across thermal cycles.

Aerodynamic & Structural Modifications

Objective: Mount high-downforce aerodynamic carbon fiber elements while safeguarding thin composite panels against shear tear-out.

Execution: Engineered load-spreading aluminum backing plates and calculated fastener shear distribution under dynamic buffeting loads.

Outcome: Successfully distributed aerodynamic downforce with a calculated $1.85$ safety factor margin on shear loads.
